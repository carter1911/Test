Meet the Artist

About Fifi &
Her Salon

Fifi is more than a hairstylist — she is an artist with a passion for protective styling and healthy hair.

With over 20 years of professional braiding experience, Fifi has built a reputation in the DMV area for her gentle touch, meticulous technique, and genuine care for every client's hair health.

What sets Fifi apart is her background in trichology — the science of hair and scalp care. This expertise allows her to tailor every style to protect your natural hair while creating breathtaking results. Whether you're tender-headed or have fine, delicate strands, Fifi ensures a pain-free, comfortable experience every time.

Today, Fifi leads a talented team of skilled braiders at 2338 Columbia Pike, Arlington, VA, all sharing the same commitment to professionalism, artistry, and client satisfaction. The salon's clean, boutique atmosphere makes every visit feel like a luxury retreat — not just a hair appointment.

"My purpose has always been to bring confidence and comfort to each guest — one braid at a time." — Fifi, Owner & Master Braider
